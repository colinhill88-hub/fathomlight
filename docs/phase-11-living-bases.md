## Habitat systems and the first implementation

- A room-side Habitat Console opens management consistently, including when the diver carries a specimen or the tray is full. Jar transfer is an explicit action in management. Water status, feeding, and cleaning are grouped at the top of the panel. The Phase 10 indoor rigs and breeding and growth information remain in the shared care system.
- The separate amber tray gives one direct Collect action through the existing inventory transfer. Tray art shows waiting output, and the prompt reflects the item count. Full bags leave the tray intact. Partial collection reports the remaining contents. A Grand tank has a console on each accessible deck and one output tray on its lower deck.
- `?bases=original` restores the shared tank interaction and older panel layout. The new entry points reuse `CareMode`, `takeTray`, `depositJar`, and the existing on-foot Collect action. Save fields, tank capacity, care costs, inventory caps, production rates, pipe supply, and conveyor behavior use the existing rules.
- `world/tanklife.ts` already renders the same populations inside and outside. Its water box handles plants, moving creatures, per-tank flakes, scrub bubbles, glass, and murk. This is the rendering boundary for feeding, cleaning, and water upgrades. `game/creatures.ts` owns the care economy, hatching, and breeding. `game/factory.ts` owns output, conveyors and feed pipes.
